A little help with your learning.

Questions about Baro? Here are a few quick answers, and a way to reach us when you need more help.

Contact support

barosomaliapp@gmail.com

Tell us what happened, your app version and your device model. For lesson or audio feedback, include the word or phrase, dialect and companion you selected. Please never send your password.

Change your dialect or audio voice

Open Settings to choose Hargeisa or Mogadishu. Choose Farah or Ikram to change your companion and the voice used for word audio.

Audio is missing or sounds wrong

Check your device volume and whether sound is playing through headphones or a Bluetooth device, then tap the speaker again. If a recording still seems incorrect, email us the word or phrase and the dialect and companion you selected.

Review words you find tricky

Open Practice for flashcards and listening exercises. Complete a lesson to unlock review of what you have learned, and flag difficult words to revisit them.

Where is my progress saved?

Learning progress is stored on your device. It does not currently sync between devices. Removing the app or clearing its data may remove your local progress.

Account and privacy help

Open Settings and then Your account to manage your account or request deletion in the app. If you cannot access your account or need help with your data, contact barosomaliapp@gmail.com.
